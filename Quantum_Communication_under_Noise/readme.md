# Quantum Communication Under Noise

An independent quantum computing project exploring how noise affects entanglement-based quantum communication using Python and Qiskit.

## Overview
Quantum entanglement is a fundamental resource for quantum communication protocols. However, environmental noise and imperfect quantum operations can degrade entanglement and affect communication reliability.
  
This project aims to simulate quantum communication under different noise models and investigate their effects on entanglement fidelity and communication performance.

## Tools
- Python
- Qiskit
- qBraid

## Objectives
- Implement and verify foundational quantum circuits, starting with Bell-state preparation.
- Simulate quantum noise channels, including depolarizing and amplitude damping. (customized noise model or mimic a real device)
- Evaluate the effects of noise on entanglement fidelity.
- Visualize and compare results across different noise models and parameters.

## Future Directions
- Evaluate strategies for improving communication reliability
